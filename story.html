Our beginning

A coffee shop
that feels like a pause.

Veloura began in 2016 with a simple idea: coffee should be more than a drink; it should be a moment. A small ritual between one thing and the next, made with enough care to make you stay awhile.

We built our first room around that feeling — warm light, a generous table, and coffee that rewards the time it takes to drink it.

02

Our beans

From careful hands
to your cup.

We select Arabica beans for their clarity, sweetness, and sense of place. Our sourcing stays origin-focused, working with growers and small lots that tell a distinct story in every harvest.

Each coffee is roasted in small batches, close to service, so its freshness and character arrive exactly as intended.
